Open-access papers related to Bluetooth Mesh and Bluetooth Low Energy.

Paywalled publisher PDFs (most IEEE conference and journal papers) were not downloaded.
The files here are legal open-access copies from arXiv, MDPI, author pages, and the Bluetooth SIG.

INDEX.csv lists the indexed PDFs.
NOT_DOWNLOADED.csv lists further relevant records whose PDF link did not resolve from this machine.

Tiers in INDEX.csv:
1  Bluetooth Mesh plus security, provisioning, attacks, privacy, or formal analysis
2  other Bluetooth Mesh papers (protocol, energy, routing, performance)
3  Bluetooth or BLE security, pairing, privacy, fuzzing, or state-machine papers
4  other open Bluetooth or BLE papers (ranging, positioning, applications)

A few extra PDFs in this folder use the publisher's own filename and are not in INDEX.csv.
